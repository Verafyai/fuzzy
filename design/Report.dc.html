Withdraw accepts a vault the caller doesn't own

Missing owner check, account substitution

The withdraw instruction checks that the vault account has the right layout but never checks which program owns it. An attacker passes a look-alike vault they created, with a balance they set, and withdraws real SOL from the pool against it.

  1. Create an account with the vault's layout, owned by the attacker's program.
  2. Write a balance of 3.2 SOL into it.
  3. Call withdraw with the fake vault and the real pool.
